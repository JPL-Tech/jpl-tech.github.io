Who it's for

People who plan their year on a tablet, a computer, or on paper printed at home: students, professionals who plan the week, parents running a household calendar, and anyone who wants a full 2027 planner without building one.

The problem

A paper planner has to be bought, carried and cannot be searched. A plain PDF calendar makes you scroll through pages to find a month. This planner has a yearly view, a page for each month and a page for each week. Every month and every week is one tap away from the index.

How to use

  1. Download the file for your device (Letter for a computer or print, Tablet for a tablet).
  2. Open it in a PDF app. Start on the Index.
  3. Tap a month to open it, then tap a day to open its week. Prev and Next move one week at a time.
  4. Write with a pen, a keyboard, or in a PDF annotation app. The lines are fixed for the year.

Note on links: the internal links were checked in the file itself (761 links, all pointing to a page in the planner). We have not yet tested them in a specific tablet app, and some PDF apps do not follow internal links. If yours does not, use the page list or bookmarks of the app.

FAQ

Does it work on an iPad or Android tablet? Yes, as a PDF. The Tablet file is built for tablet screens. The Letter file also works on a tablet, with smaller text.

Can I print it? Yes. The Letter file is built for 8.5 × 11 in paper. Links only work on screen; the layout prints as designed.

Are the holidays right? The planner marks the US federal holidays for 2027 on their observed dates (for example, Juneteenth on Friday, June 18, and Independence Day on Monday, July 5).

Is there a version for 2028? Not yet. This version is only for 2027.

Can I share it or resell it? No. It is for one person's personal use. Please do not share the file or sell it.

What format is it? PDF only. There is no Excel or Google Sheets version in this product.

What if the links don't work in my app? Use the page list or bookmarks of your PDF app. The layout works the same either way.
